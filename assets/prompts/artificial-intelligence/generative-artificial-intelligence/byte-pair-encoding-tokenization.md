# recvw24KNremp6 · Byte-Pair Encoding Tokenization

Version: v1 · alignment: unverified

A 30-second silent English concept animation for a university Generative Artificial Intelligence course: Byte-Pair Encoding Tokenization. Start from individual characters, count adjacent pairs across a tiny corpus, merge the leading pair and recount, then replay the learned ranks on “lowest” so the common stem compresses while the suffix stays split.

Light ground `#ffffff` -> `#f6f8fb` -> `#eaeff6`; primary `#009e73` on the main symbols; accent `#b87f00` on the selected pair; result `#c0729d` on the learned chunks; warn `#d55e00` on a low-frequency or unmatched fragment; “Helvetica Neue”, Helvetica, Arial, “Segoe UI”, sans-serif throughout, 700-800 headings, 21px uppercase kickers at 5px letter-spacing. Use draw-on + stagger-in — adjacent character pairs combine in ranked order and learned chunks replay across a new word, with 12f fades, offsets stepping 8-14f, and nothing landing in a scene’s last 25 frames.

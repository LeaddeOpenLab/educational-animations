motion style are. That is the whole card — it is meant to be pasted into another
model as-is, so it carries the exact hex values rather than a reference to them.

All 12 share one palette (`okabe-ito-dark`)
because the palette is the visual identity of the batch, not of a single video.
To re-skin the set, replace the colour sentence in every card with another palette
from `assets/palettes.json`; nothing else changes.

Generated by `scripts/emit-prompt-cards.mjs` — frame budgets are read from the
video source; final MP4 duration and visual alignment still require verification.

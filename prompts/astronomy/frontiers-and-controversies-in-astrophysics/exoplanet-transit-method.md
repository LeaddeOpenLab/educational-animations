# C20-A001 · Exoplanet transit method

Version: v1 · alignment: unverified

A 30-second silent English concept animation for a university astronomy course about Exoplanet transit method: Infer a planet radius from a repeatable stellar brightness dip; a planet crosses a star, obscuring area; fractional depth is approximately (planet radius / stellar radius)^2.

Dark backgrounds #0b0d0f to #13191f to #1c242c; primary #828231 for the main data, accent #d29343 for the key quantity, result #fdac9e for the inference, and warn #d55e00 for caveats; Helvetica Neue headings and labels; draw-on paths over 40–60 frames, stagger-in labels over 12 frames, and finish every element at least 25 frames before each scene ends.
